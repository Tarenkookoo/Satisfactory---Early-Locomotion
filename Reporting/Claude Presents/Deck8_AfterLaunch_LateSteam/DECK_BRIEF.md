## Easy to get wrong

- **Slide 3, 4, 5, 8** are Agreed principles. **Slides 6, 7** list ideas that are Proposed or open: keep their chips. **Never** show steam with electric's full abilities.
- **Slides 9 to 15** (advanced driving) are Agreed in scope. Exact formulas, the size of any Advanced advantage and screens are open, so their inline Undecided chips stay. Boiler explosions, permanent damage and engine destruction are **not** features: slide 14 says so.
- **Slide 15:** Simple must not look crippled. Advanced is only "a little" better and the amount is undecided.
- **Slide 16 and 17:** Agreed principles. **Slide 18:** Proposed and unchecked.
- **Slides 19 to 21:** Agreed. Name no real engine, number or price. The slide 19 inline Background chip explains a game fact only.
- **Slide 22:** Proposed. There is no passenger economy.
- **Slide 23:** every item is "an idea". Nothing is "under way".
