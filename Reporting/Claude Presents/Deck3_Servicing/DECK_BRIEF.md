## Easy to get wrong

- **Slide 4:** water is "usually" the tighter limit (Agreed). Show no coal-to-water ratio.
- **Slide 10:** every number is a Playtest Target. The Provisional chip stays and the footer line stays.
- **Slide 11:** do not claim Compacted Coal saves water. The design says the water saving was not established.
- **Slide 12 and 13:** station size is undecided. Do not give dimensions.
- **Slides 14 and 15:** the retraction step ("Machinery folds away") and the target-point names are Proposed; keep those chips visible. The names are suggestions, not existing game features.
- **Slide 16:** "5 to 15 extra seconds" is an example, so it carries Provisional. Real transfer rates are only a later idea.
- **Slides 17 and 18:** the facility set and the water-only timetable stop are Proposed. Automatic servicing is undecided.
- **Slide 19:** the one-vehicle model and both auxiliary-tender types are Proposed. Normal tender versus auxiliary tender is Agreed.
- **Slide 22:** only the states "Under way" and "Planned". No technical test detail, and nothing that implies coal, water or servicing already work.
- **Slide 23:** questions only.
