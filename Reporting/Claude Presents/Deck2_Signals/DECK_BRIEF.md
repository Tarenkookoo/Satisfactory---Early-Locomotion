## Easy to get wrong

- **Slide 8:** the caption begins "On real railways". The mod's failure behaviour is undecided, so the Undecided chip stays.
- **Slide 6:** whether the arm rises or drops when clear is undecided. Do not pick one for the mod; the diagram shows a raised arm only as an example of "clear".
- **Slide 18:** all four numbers are provisional; each tile has its own Provisional chip.
- **Slide 21:** shown as Proposed on purpose (the design file does not label the box-screen contents as Agreed). Say in `BUILD_NOTES.md` that the owner may upgrade the tag.
- **Slides 22, 23 and 24:** keep each inline Proposed chip visible. Do not present destination routing, improved semaphores or electronic signals as decided.
- **Slides 20 and 25:** every R03 and R04 picture carries "Blender model, not yet in the game."
- **Slide 25:** only the states "Under way" and "Planned". No technical test detail.
- **Slide 26:** questions only.
