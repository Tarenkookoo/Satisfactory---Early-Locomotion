## Easy to get wrong

- **Slides 3 and 4:** steam and monorail are physically incompatible (Agreed). The older idea that converted monorail can still carry steam is Superseded and never appears.
- **Slides 7 to 14:** only "route kept" (slide 7 headline) and "cannot cross" (inline chip, slide 14) are Agreed. Everything else about stations, signals, networks and the interchange is Proposed or Undecided.
- **Slide 9:** do not promise that shapes survive. **Slides 16 and 17:** show questions only. Give no costs, refunds or lengths.
- **Slide 15:** the Transfer Station is a possibility, not a launch feature.
- **Slide 20:** only "Under way" and "Planned". No technical test detail.
- Tag counts to check: Agreed 6 (slides 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 19), Proposed 6 (8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14), Undecided 5 (9, 15, 16, 17, 21), Provisional 2 (18, 20), no chip 3 (1, 2, 22).
