## Easy to get wrong

- **The programme is Proposed and post-launch.** The Agreed items are only the principles named on the slides marked A. Never say "will". Say "could" where the captions do.
- **Slide 3 and 4:** Core is never weakened to sell packs (Agreed). Expansions share one foundation (Agreed). Do not mention a physics engine of any other kind.
- **Slide 5 and 6:** no price, no pack names, no engine counts. The roster counts in the design (four shunters, and so on) must not appear anywhere.
- **Slide 8:** category names are candidates only. **Slide 15:** no dates and no historical claims beyond the five operator badges.
- **Slides 17 and 18** are Proposed. **Slide 19** is an Agreed fallback with open matching rules.
- **Slide 20:** a hope, not a feature. Multiplayer comes after release.
- **Slide 22:** only the states in the table, never "under way" for anything in this deck.
